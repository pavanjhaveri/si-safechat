# Sample: Amazon shopping FAQ

> Demo snapshot for trying SI SafeChat. Based on public Amazon help topics · Snapshot: October 2026 · Paraphrased demo content — see Amazon's official help pages for authoritative answers.

## How do returns work?

Most items bought from Amazon can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund. You start the return from your order history, choose a reason, and select a return method such as a drop-off point or a carrier pickup. Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the return is received and inspected.

## What shipping options are there?

Amazon offers standard, expedited, and (where available) same-day or one-day delivery. Shipping speed and cost depend on the item, your location, and whether you have Prime. Some orders over a minimum amount qualify for free standard shipping without Prime.

## What is Amazon Prime?

Prime is a paid membership that includes fast free delivery on eligible items, plus extras like Prime Video streaming, Prime Music, and exclusive deals. Membership can be monthly or annual, and there is usually a free trial for new members.

## How long do refunds take?

After Amazon receives your return, refunds typically take 3–5 business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer. You can track the refund status in your account under the returns section.

## How do I track my order?

Every shipped order gets a tracking number visible in your order details. You can follow the package from the carrier facility to your door, and Amazon sends delivery notifications along the way.

## What if my package says delivered but it isn't?

First check around your property, with neighbors, and with other household members. If it still doesn't turn up after a day or two, contact Amazon support through your order page — they can investigate, replace, or refund missing packages.

## How do I change or cancel an order?

You can change the shipping address or speed, or cancel items, from your order details — but only before the order enters the shipping process. Once an item has shipped, cancellation is no longer possible and you would use a return instead.

## Is my payment information safe?

Amazon encrypts payment details and never shares your full card number with third-party sellers. You can also use gift cards, and manage or remove saved payment methods in your account settings.

## What are Amazon's customer service hours?

Amazon's customer support is available 24/7 through chat and phone in most regions. You can reach them from the "Help" or "Contact Us" section of the site or app.

## How do digital purchases work?

Digital items like Kindle books, apps, and Prime Video rentals are delivered instantly to your library or device. Accidental digital purchases can usually be cancelled or refunded within a short window from your digital orders page.
